SUPPORT

Help Center

Answers to common questions about using InstaLearn.

How do I add a subject?

Go to My Subjects and use the search and filters to find a subject, then tap it to add it to your dashboard.

How is my mastery / progress calculated?

Mastery is based on the topics you've completed and how well you scored on their quizzes. Check the Progress tab for a full breakdown per subject.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your registered email. You can also change your password anytime from Settings while logged in.

How do I change my display name or photo?

Go to Settings → Profile to upload a display photo and update your display name.

Is my data saved if I use a different device?

Your account (login) is powered by Firebase, but progress and profile details are currently stored on this browser/device as part of the prototype.
